# DuckyGuard Brand Specification
**A product by sh. industries · Engineered by Steven Hainley**

DuckyGuard adopts the **sh. industries** design philosophy: *clean, premium, dark-matte restraint with retro pixel craft reserved strictly for the mascot and eyebrow kickers.*

---

## 1. Visual Identity & Brand Pillars

1. **Pixel Mascot ("Ducky")**:
   - The mascot is the **only** element that is fully 16-bit pixelated.
   - Design: Black/ink silhouette with crisp off-white edges, sporting a tactical glowing **Cyber Amber** (`#FF7A00`) visor and beak.
   - Always rendered with `image-rendering: pixelated;` to preserve crisp edges without bilinear smoothing.
2. **Restraint & Cyberpunk Realism**:
   - No cheesy CRT scanlines or messy 90s hacker green.
   - Dark matte obsidian paper (`#0E0E0D`), clean monospace telemetry, and focused amber accents.
3. **Typography**:
   - **Headings / Wordmark**: *Funnel Display* / *SF Pro Display* (Weight: 700/800, tight tracking).
   - **Eyebrow Kickers**: *Departure Mono* / *Geist Pixel* (Uppercase, tracking: `0.18em`, 11–13px).
   - **Terminal & Forensics**: *Menlo* / *SF Mono* (Weight: 500).
