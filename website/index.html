Multi-Layer Interception

Engineered to outrun microsecond keystrokes.

Hardware injection attacks rely on the operating system blindly trusting any device that identifies as a human keyboard. DuckyGuard breaks that trust assumption.

01 / IOKIT SENTINEL

USB Hardware Bus Sweeper

Continuously polls macOS IOKit hardware controller registries. Correlates external keyboard attachment timestamps with immediate typing activity, flagging devices that begin burst typing within seconds of enumeration.

02 / BIOMETRIC HEURISTICS

Keystroke Dynamics & Jitter

Evaluates rolling typing speed (CPS) against human inter-keystroke interval (IKI) timing variance. Microcontroller delay loops flatline near 0ms jitter; human key spamming naturally generates high biomechanical entropy.

03 / ACTIVE CONTAINMENT

Focus-Stealing Decoy Sink

When an anomaly confidence exceeds threshold, DuckyGuard instantly seizes WindowServer focus into a secure containment sandbox. Keystrokes are sunk harmlessly to disk before commands can reach Terminal or bash.

04 / THREAT SYNTHESIS

Automated YARA & MITRE Mapping

Captured payloads are parsed for malicious shell patterns, reverse shells, and download cradles. The forensic analyzer automatically maps MITRE ATT&CK TTPs (T1200, T1059.001) and compiles deployable YARA rules.
